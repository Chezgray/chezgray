The Suite

A place to rest,
a reason to give.

We've opened our downstairs guest suite to friends and family who need a comfortable place to stay. It's our way of sharing what we've been given — and we ask only one thing in return: a small donation to one of the causes close to our hearts.

The suite is fully private with its own entrance, and is designed to feel like a real home — not a hotel. You'll have everything you need for a restful stay.

Our Home

House Rules

We want you to feel at home — and we want our home to stay a welcoming place for every guest who comes after you.

01

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in is after 3 PM, check-out by 11 AM. Early or late arrangements welcome — just let us know in advance.

02

Guests

The suite sleeps up to 2 guests. Please don't bring additional overnight visitors without checking with us first.

03

Quiet Hours

Please keep noise to a minimum between 10 PM and 8 AM. We're a residential home with neighbours nearby.

04

No Smoking

No smoking anywhere on the property, including outside. We appreciate your understanding.

05

Leave It as You Found It

Please tidy up before you leave — dishes washed, towels left in the bathroom, rubbish in the bin.

06

The Donation

We ask guests to make a small donation to one of our supported charities. Any amount is meaningful and deeply appreciated.
